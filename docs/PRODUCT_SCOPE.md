# ChemLearn Product Scope

## Product

ChemLearn is not only a virtual chemistry laboratory.

It is a complete interactive chemistry learning platform.

## V1

The initial curriculum is Chemistry 9.

Students will be able to:

1. follow a guided Chemistry 9 course
2. learn concepts using visual explanations
3. interact with scientific models
4. perform curriculum-relevant virtual experiments
5. practise chemistry questions
6. complete chapter assessments
7. track concept-level mastery
8. switch between English and Roman Urdu

## Future

Chemistry 10 will be added after the Chemistry 9 platform and reusable science systems are stable.

## Learning Model

Lessons should generally follow:

See → Understand → Interact → Apply → Check → Master

## Scope Rule

ChemLearn should be:

- academically serious
- visually understandable
- interactive
- curriculum aligned

It should not be:

- a simplistic children's animation app
- a university-level chemistry simulator
- only a collection of experiments
- an AI chatbot
- a copied digital textbook

## Course and Lab Relationship

Experiments are learning tools inside the course.

The same scientific platform may later support a Free ChemLab mode for open exploration.

## AI

AI/RAG is not required for the initial core build.

Future AI may explain approved curriculum content.

AI must not determine:

- chemistry calculations
- reaction outcomes
- correct answers
- mastery
- assessment scoring
